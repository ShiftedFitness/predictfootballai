How it works


    
  
  • The game rotates: Higher or Lower, Who Am I?, Player Alphabet, Trivia
    Quiz, Starting XI, then round again.
    • 
  
  • The club is drawn from hundreds of team-and-competition
    combinations, every one of which has enough history behind it to make a
    fair round.
    • 
  
  • Your streak is kept in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere and you do
    not need an account to start one.
    • 
  
  • Shared results are blocks, never names — whoever you send it to can still
    play the same day.
