Football games and quizzes built from real player records

6 games. 40,148 players, 355 clubs, 12 competitions. How good are you?

Today’s challenge

One puzzle a day, the same for everyone — plus what others are playing and what they have built.

All 6 games

Every one uses the same real records. Pick a club or a competition, or just play.

  • Bullseye Pick players. Reach zero.
    You start on 501. Name a player and their appearances come off your total. Land exactly on zero — going under puts the score back.
  • Starting XI Build your ultimate eleven.
    Choose a formation, then fill every position with a player from the club or competition you picked. Your score is the total of whichever stat you are playing for.
  • Higher or Lower Who played more games?
    Two players at a time. Say which one made more appearances, and keep going until you get one wrong.
  • Who Am I? Five clues. One footballer.
    A mystery player, revealed one clue at a time. The sooner you name them, the more you score.
  • Player Alphabet A player for every letter.
    A to Z, one player per letter. Letters nobody has a player for are skipped.
  • Trivia Quiz Ten questions. How many?
    Ten questions drawn from real records for the club or competition you chose.
